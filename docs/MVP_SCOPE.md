# MVP Scope

## Product scope
1. Universal sales intelligence
2. Sales decision support
3. Customer communication
4. Negotiation and closing
5. Existing-customer management

## First workflow
Explicitly authorized active conversation context → intent/needs/buying-stage/objection analysis → verify product and commercial facts → strategy and reply draft → quality and risk review → human review → copy and manual send → approved customer-summary update.

## Pilot
Start with 2–3 users. Measure reliability, usefulness, latency, review/edit rate, and user-reported outcomes before planning expansion toward 1,000 users.

## Not in MVP
Lead generation, prospect discovery, bulk contact collection, brand management, ERP/MES/WMS, manufacturing, and factory operations.
